# Verikord EU Shop Duties Check

A plugin for Claude that tells an online merchant which EU duties apply to
their shop, since when or from when, and what to do first.

## What it does

Claude asks up to eight short questions about the shop: where the customers
are, where the company is based, who buys, what is sold, own brand or own
import, company size, use of AI, and environmental claims. It then checks a
dated list of EU rules and returns a short checklist with the official or
chamber source for every rule.

The list covers the withdrawal button, the EU labels for legal guarantee and
commercial guarantee (GARAN), product safety details in listings (GPSR),
packaging (PPWR), accessibility, the right to repair, AI transparency,
environmental claims, discount advertising, product liability for importers,
e-invoicing in Germany, the deforestation regulation (EUDR), the battery
passport, the digital product passport and textile producer responsibility.
For EU directives it shows the German implementation.

## How to use it

Install the plugin, then ask Claude in your own words. Three examples:

- "Which EU duties apply to my Shopify store? I sell my own clothing brand
  from Switzerland to customers in Germany and France."
- "Welche Pflichten und Fristen gelten für meinen Onlineshop? Ich verkaufe
  Kaffee an Privatkunden in Deutschland."
- "Do I need a battery passport for the e-bikes I import, and from when?"

Claude answers in the language you write in. English and German wording is
included.

## Limits

The plugin gives general information, not legal advice. Every result ends
with that disclosure and the date of the data. Rules and deadlines change.
Have a lawyer or your chamber of commerce confirm the result before you act
on it. The plugin never states that a shop is compliant.

## Data

The plugin contains instructions and a reference list only. It has no
connector, runs no code, stores nothing and sends nothing to Verikord or to
anyone else. Your answers stay in your conversation with Claude. Verikord's
privacy policy is linked in the footer of https://verikord.com.

## Updates

The reference list states the date on which it was last checked. New versions
are published when dates or rules change.
